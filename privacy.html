Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

This policy explains what information YAT-C ("we", "the game") handles when you use this website.

Information we collect

  • Account data. If you sign up, we store your email address and a password hash with our authentication provider, Supabase.
  • Game data. Your worlds (terrain and placed pieces), world names, extra world slots and token balance are stored in your account. Guests' worlds are stored only in the browser on their device.
  • Usage and device data. Our hosting and advertising partners may receive standard technical data such as IP address, browser type and pages visited.

How we use it

We use this information to run the game, save your progress, prevent abuse, show advertising and improve the game. We do not sell your personal information.

Advertising and cookies

We use Google AdSense to show ads, including optional rewarded ads. Google and its partners use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can learn how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and opt out of personalised advertising at adssettings.google.com. You can also visit aboutads.info to opt out of some third-party vendors' cookies.

Local storage

The game stores settings and guest worlds in your browser's local storage. Clearing your browser data removes them.

Third-party services

We rely on Supabase (accounts and storage), Render (hosting), Google Fonts and Google AdSense. Each has its own privacy policy.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete your account and saved worlds at any time by contacting us at EMAIL.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
