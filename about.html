About

YAT-C, short for You And The City, is an independent browser game about building a place of your own and then living in it.

Why we made it

City builders are usually about spreadsheets and traffic. We wanted something more personal: a game where you design a single street corner, the shop on it and the light above the door, then walk down that street yourself. Every piece you place, from roofs to road markings, is chosen by you.

How it works

The game runs entirely in your web browser using WebGL, so there is nothing to install. Your worlds are saved on your device, or in your account if you sign in, so you can return to them from any device.

What is next

We are working on letting players share worlds and visit each other's cities, and on building together in shared sessions. Feedback shapes what we build next, so please get in touch.
