You And The City

YAT-C is a free 3D city builder that runs in your browser. Shape the land, lay out streets, design your own buildings, then step inside and walk the city you made.

Play now

What you can do

  • Sculpt the terrain. Raise hills, dig valleys and paint grass, sand, rock or snow across a voxel landscape.
  • Build roads and rails. Choose road surfaces and pavements, and connect your districts with streets and transit lines.
  • Design buildings. Pick facades, windows, doors, roofs, balconies and cornices, set the number of floors, and add LED billboards and signs.
  • Plant forests and walls. Add trees in different shapes, fences and garden walls with posts, caps and gates.
  • Set the weather and sky. Change the time of day and watch lights come on at night, with rain and snow when you want them.
  • Save your worlds. Sign in to keep several worlds in the cloud, or play as a guest and save on your device.

Two ways to play

Build mode gives you a free camera and a palette of pieces. Place them on a grid, rotate them, and edit any building in detail with the inspector.

Roam mode drops you into your city at street level. Walk around, look up at your skyline and explore. Choose Peaceful for quiet exploration, Normal where only hazards can hurt you, or Survival where the city fights back.

Works on phone and desktop

YAT-C adapts to your device. On a computer you use the mouse and keyboard. On a phone or tablet you get on-screen controls for the camera and for walking.

Free to play

The game is free. Players can watch an optional rewarded ad to earn tokens and unlock extra world slots. Read how we handle data and advertising in the Privacy Policy, or learn more about the project.
